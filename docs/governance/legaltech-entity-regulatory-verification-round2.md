## Executive result

Round 2 confirms that the Jordanian corporate-registry system provides public electronic inquiry mechanisms capable of searching companies by company name, partner name, registration number/type, national establishment number, registered purposes, trade name, and other fields. The Companies Control Department also exposes legislation, interpretive decisions, policies, governance guidance, and beneficial-owner information through its official site.

However, the public web interface available to this research run did **not** return a verifiable company-record result for Huqouk or Qada.ai. The registry search endpoints either timed out or returned gateway errors during this run. Therefore **ownership, legal entity, registered purposes, registration number, and beneficial-owner information for either operator remain UNVERIFIED** in this evidence register.

This is an evidence limitation, not evidence that either operator is unregistered or non-compliant.

## 3. Official Companies Control Department evidence

The Companies Control Department's official website provides an electronic company-inquiry service. It explicitly offers searches by company name, partner name, company number/type, national establishment number, registered purposes, trade name, and other corporate attributes. citeturn2view0

The same official site publishes corporate legislation, regulations, instructions, interpretive decisions, policies, governance foundations, and beneficial-owner information. citeturn0view4

The current research run could reach the inquiry service but could not retrieve a stable company-record result for the benchmark operators because the relevant portal endpoints timed out/returned gateway errors. Accordingly, no ownership or registered-purpose claim is recorded here without a returned official record.

## 5. New Mustasharek evidence rule

A benchmark operator may be used to learn **how a market participant structures its public product and contracts**. It may not be used to infer that the same model is licensed, tax-compliant, Bar-approved, or permissible for Mustasharek.

The next entity-verification attempt should obtain, where possible, a first-party corporate identifier or official registry extract from the operator itself, then independently match it against the Companies Control Department record. For payment, tax and professional-regulation questions, separate evidence must still be obtained from the relevant competent authority or qualified Jordanian counsel.
