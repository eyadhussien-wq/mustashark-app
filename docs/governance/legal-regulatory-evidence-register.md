## Liability Test A/B/C/D

**A — Professional Liability:** legal advice and professional execution by the licensed provider, subject to the actual law and contract.  
**B — Platform Liability:** platform conduct, technical performance, representations and consumer-facing obligations.  
**C — Data Liability:** personal data, confidentiality, access control, processing, retention and security obligations.  
**D — Payment Liability:** collection, settlement, refunds and any regulated payment activity.

## Round 1 official evidence anchors

### Payment

The Central Bank of Jordan's official licensing materials state that payment/electronic transfer activities and operation/management of electronic payment systems are subject to the applicable licensing framework.

## Important legal-design rule

Terms of use, disclaimers, and checkboxes cannot create immunity that the law does not permit. They can document an allocation of responsibility only to the extent that allocation is legally valid.

## Research discipline

A competitor's existence, licensing, public website wording, or continued operation is **MARKET OBSERVATION**, not proof that Mustasharek's proposed model is lawful. Each material legal conclusion must be independently validated.
