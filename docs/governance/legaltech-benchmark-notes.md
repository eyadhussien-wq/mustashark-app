# Jordan LegalTech Benchmark Notes

**Status:** Read-only market research baseline

## Purpose

Record public-market examples discussed during discovery so future work can distinguish real-world operating patterns from legal approval.

## Examples discussed

### Huqouk / منصة حقوق

**Classification:** MARKET OBSERVATION — requires independent verification of current legal entity, contractual structure, payment flow, and regulatory status.

The discussion identified it as an example of a digital legal-services marketplace/intermediary model connecting users with lawyers and presenting the platform as distinct from a traditional law firm. These observations must not be treated as proof that an identical Mustasharek model is approved.

### Qada.ai

**Classification:** MARKET OBSERVATION — different operating model from a pure lawyer marketplace.

The discussion identified it primarily as a legal technology/intelligence and workflow platform with lawyer-in-the-loop concepts. It is useful as a LegalTech benchmark but should not be treated as direct evidence for the legality of Mustasharek's marketplace/payment model.

## Benchmark questions for each future example

1. Legal entity and jurisdiction.
2. Registered business activities.
3. Whether the platform describes itself as a law firm, technology company, marketplace, intermediary, or another category.
4. Who contracts with the end client.
5. Who provides the legal service.
6. How lawyers are onboarded and verified.
7. How pricing and professional fees are determined.
8. Platform commission/fee structure.
9. Payment provider and settlement structure, where publicly disclosed.
10. Invoice issuer and tax treatment, where publicly disclosed.
11. Refund/cancellation mechanics.
12. Terms and liability allocation.
13. Privacy/data-processing commitments.
14. Advertising and lawyer-profile rules.
15. Whether any regulator, Bar, licence, or official approval is expressly identified.

## Evidence rule

Do not record claims such as “licensed”, “approved”, “legal”, “PSP”, “escrow”, or “tax-compliant” unless an authoritative source establishes the specific fact. Public website wording is evidence of what the operator claims, not independent regulatory approval.

## Benchmark conclusion

Market examples are valuable for designing questions and identifying possible operating patterns. They do **not** substitute for a Jordan-specific legal, tax, payment, privacy, and professional-regulation assessment of Mustasharek's exact model.
